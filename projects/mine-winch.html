Machine Design

Mine Haulage Winch

The belt drive, wire rope, roller chain, cable drum, drum shaft, bearings and chassis of a winch that hauls a 5-tonne cart up a 25° mine tunnel.

ReportRepository
Course
Machine Elements Design II (Project 4)
Term
Fall 2025
Team
Alireza Amjadi, Seyed Mohammad Amin Bidgoli
Tools
  • SolidWorks

Overview

Select the belts, wire rope and chain, and design the drum, drum shaft, bearings and chassis of a winch that hauls up to 5 t up a 150 m tunnel at 25°, in 5-minute cycles, from an 11 kW motor and the gearbox of Project 3.

Approach

  1. Static analysis of the cart on the incline gives the rope tension; the effective power after the transmission losses gives the cart speed.
  2. V-belt drive sized from the design power and the speed ratio; two trials lead to three B-125 belts.
  3. Wire rope selected for static strength and bending fatigue over the drum and sheave; drum sized for two rope layers.
  4. Duplex roller chain from the gearbox to the drum; drum shaft in fatigue; self-aligning bearings; welded chassis with adjustable motor and gearbox seats.
