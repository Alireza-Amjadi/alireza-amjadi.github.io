09Structural Design

Fork-Mounted Jib Crane

A demountable crane for a 3-tonne forklift: boom sections, pins, lifting lugs, fillet welds and bolted joints, with manufacturing drawings, a bill of materials and a user catalogue.

ReportCatalogueRepository
Course
Machine Elements Design I
Term
Spring 2025
Team
Alireza Amjadi, Seyed Mohammad Amin Bidgoli
Tools
  • SolidWorks
  • MATLAB

Overview

What profiles, pins, welds and bolts let the crane carry the forklift's allowable load at full reach and in its worst position, with a safety factor of 4.5 on the structure and 5 on the pins?

Approach

  1. Allowable hook load from the forklift's rated moment; inertia loads from the forklift's accelerations.
  2. Principal stresses on the critical element at the pin hole, horizontal and 45° positions.
  3. Pins in shear and bearing; fillet welds of the lugs and plates; bolt preload and tightening torque.
