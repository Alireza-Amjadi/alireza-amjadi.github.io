3D PINN for Pipe Flow

A physics-informed neural network for the 3D Navier–Stokes equations in a pipe, with exact wall and outlet conditions, written from scratch in NumPy with its own automatic differentiation.

Overview

Can a PINN recover the development of laminar flow in a pipe from the governing equations alone, and how close does it get to the exact fully developed solution?

Approach

  1. Inputs (r cos θ, r sin θ, z), so every field is periodic in θ and regular on the axis; residuals in Cartesian form.
  2. No-slip and outlet-pressure conditions imposed exactly by distance functions; a blunt inlet profile that is compatible with the wall.
  3. Re = 10, chosen so that the flow develops inside the pipe and a valid exact reference exists.
  4. 4 × 50 tanh network; 3000 Adam iterations, then four L-BFGS rounds on resampled points.
